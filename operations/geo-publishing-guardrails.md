# RaidBench GEO Publishing Guardrails

Last updated: 2026-08-10

## Approval Levels

### Green: Can Publish After Checklist

- Owned website guide page.
- Owned calculator or scorecard update.
- Owned methodology or source-note update.
- Sitemap, robots, or internal-link update.
- Public guide index update.

### Yellow: Draft First, Owner Review Before Publishing

- Reddit answer that mentions RaidBench.
- Steam reply that references RaidBench or any external page.
- Discord post in a permitted resource/self-promo channel.
- Quora/forum answer with a RaidBench link.
- Creator outreach email or collaboration note.
- Any comparison involving named creators, tools, communities, or games.

### Red: Do Not Publish

- Fake testimonial or fake player story.
- Mass comments, mass DMs, or unsolicited server messages.
- Duplicate answers pasted across communities.
- Hidden affiliation when linking.
- Claims of guaranteed loot, profit, boss clears, rank, traffic, revenue, or outcomes.
- Cheats, exploit instructions, bug abuse, account sales, boosting, RMT, in-game currency, in-game items, gambling, or top-ups.
- Any post that pretends RaidBench is official or endorsed by game studios.

## Frequency Caps

| Channel | First 30 days | After 30 days | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Owned site guides | 3-5/week | 4-8/month | Main channel; low ban risk |
| Guide updates | 5/week | as needed | Triggered by patch/search data |
| Reddit comments | First 7 days: max 1/day, 24h apart | Max 2/day, 8-12h apart | Only highly relevant, unique, manual replies |
| Reddit links | First 10 helpful replies: 0 links | 1-2 links/month max | Only when rules allow; disclose affiliation |
| Steam replies | 0 links | text-only unless clearly allowed | Research-first |
| Discord posts | 0 unless permitted | channel-specific | No DMs |
| Quora/forum answers | 1-2/week drafts | 2-3/week | Link in a minority of answers |
| Creator outreach | 0-2/month | 2-4/month | Transparent collaboration only |

## Required Publishing Log

Every external post or outreach draft must be recorded:

```text
date
platform
url
game
topic
reply_level
raidbench_link
affiliation_disclosed
useful_without_click
risk_level
status
result
notes
```

Cadence is a RaidBench risk limit, not a promise that a platform will accept a post. Community-specific
rules, account history, reply quality, removals, and moderator feedback always override the numeric cap.
If one reply is removed or the account receives a warning, pause external posting for seven days and review
the cause. If two replies are removed, stop the channel until its rules and account status are re-audited.

## Review Checklist

Before publishing externally:

- The answer is useful without a click.
- The platform/community rules allow the behavior.
- Any RaidBench affiliation is disclosed if linking.
- No game outcome is guaranteed.
- No official affiliation is implied.
- No exploit/cheat/RMT/boosting/account/in-game-item content appears.
- The answer is unique to the thread.
- The frequency cap is still available.
- The post is recorded in the log.

## Safe Linking Pattern

Use:

```text
Short answer: [direct answer].

The practical checks are:
1. [check]
2. [check]
3. [check]

Disclosure: I maintain RaidBench. I wrote a free checklist/calculator for this here if you want the longer version: [link]
```

Avoid:

```text
Buy my credits here.
This will guarantee better loot/profit.
This is the best build.
Use this exploit before it gets patched.
```

## Owner Review Output

Every yellow draft should show the owner:

- English original thread/question.
- Chinese summary.
- Risk level.
- Whether link is allowed.
- English reply draft.
- Chinese explanation of the reply.
- Recommended action: publish, text-only, ask moderator, or do not reply.

## Stop Conditions

Stop using a channel when:

- a post is removed twice
- account warning appears
- moderators object
- replies call it spam
- it requires repeated link placement
- it attracts low-quality or risky traffic
