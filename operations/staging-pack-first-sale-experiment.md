## Decision Gates

| Observed signal | Interpretation | Next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fewer than 100 product-page visits and fewer than 20 previews | Sample is still too small | Continue qualified organic distribution; do not change price |
| At least 100 product-page visits but fewer than 20 previews | First viewport or free action is weak | Rewrite the hook, preset labels, and preview button |
| At least 20 previews but zero checkout starts | Paid delta, sample, or price is weak | Test paid-output framing first; then one controlled price variant |
| Checkout starts but zero report deliveries | PayPal handoff is the bottleneck | Inspect cancellation and error telemetry before changing the product |
| At least one unrelated paid report delivery | First-sale path is validated | Audit source, refund risk, support cost, and test a higher-value variant |
