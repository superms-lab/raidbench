From activity to output

Production queues, worker suitability, travel time, storage, and shortages are closely connected. RaidBench organizes those variables into a practical sequence, so the next improvement is based on evidence rather than another complete rebuild.

Guidance avoids exploit-dependent routes and is reviewed against official Palworld updates, current game information, and recurring player questions. Patch-sensitive recommendations are clearly identified for rechecking.
