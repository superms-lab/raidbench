Before you pay

What happens if RaidBench cannot verify the answer?

The request is held and no credits are charged. Custom servers and stale evidence are not treated as verified vanilla answers.

Is this a subscription?

No. Credit packs are one-time purchases in USD. Unused credits remain in the account.

Where is the review delivered?

Directly inside your RaidBench account, together with route comparisons, inventory gaps, calculations, evidence, review status, and delivery history.

Does this guarantee a profitable raid?

No. RaidBench checks the entered route and resource math; hidden layers, counters, execution, loot, and custom rules remain player risks.

Evidence and purchase terms

Paid Rust reviews use current official recipe and update sources plus an independent raid-count cross-check. The production service refuses stale or unsupported requests before charging credits.
