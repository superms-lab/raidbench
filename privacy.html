Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026. This policy explains what information RaidBench processes, why it is used, and the choices available to visitors and customers.

Scope and contact

This policy applies to raidbench.com, its calculators, guide pages, support communications, and any digital checkout displayed on the site.

Privacy requests can be sent to support@raidbench.com. The legal merchant identity for a paid transaction will also appear at checkout and in the order confirmation.

Information processed

  • Basic technical records such as request time, IP address, browser information, requested page, and security events generated by hosting infrastructure.
  • Usage events such as page views, calculator interactions, and guide clicks when an analytics service is enabled.
  • Messages, email address, and information you choose to provide when contacting support.
  • Account email, display name, selected region, a one-way password hash, session records, credit balance, requests, and delivered answers when you create an account.
  • Order reference, product, amount, currency, payment status, delivery status, and refund history when a purchase is made.

Account passwords are transformed with a salted one-way hash before storage. RaidBench never asks for a game account password or stores raw payment-card details.

Purposes and legal bases

  • Provide calculators, guides, support, purchased content, and transaction records needed to perform a contract.
  • Protect the site, prevent abuse, diagnose errors, and improve useful content based on legitimate operational interests.
  • Meet accounting, tax, dispute, fraud-prevention, and other legal obligations.
  • Use optional analytics or marketing technologies where consent is required and provided.

Analytics and cookies

RaidBench uses a first-party aggregate page-view counter to understand which public guides are useful. It records the UTC date, requested page path, referring hostname, and Cloudflare country code. The RaidBench analytics database does not store IP addresses, cookies, account identifiers, email addresses, or full referring URLs.

The counter respects Global Privacy Control and browser Do Not Track signals. Aggregate rows are retained for up to 400 days. Optional third-party analytics may be introduced later only with an updated notice and, where required, consent.

Blocking analytics does not prevent access to public calculators and guides.

Payments and service providers

If checkout is available, the payment provider shown on that checkout processes payment credentials under its own privacy notice. RaidBench receives transaction and status information needed for delivery, support, refunds, fraud checks, and records, but does not store full card numbers.

Limited data may also be processed by providers used for hosting, analytics, email delivery, file delivery, security, and customer support.

International processing

Service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where applicable, RaidBench relies on the provider's contractual safeguards and other lawful transfer mechanisms.

Retention

Technical logs are retained for a limited operational and security period. Aggregate page-view rows are retained for up to 400 days. Account, answer, and credit records remain while the account is active or are retained as needed to provide access and resolve disputes. Order, refund, tax, and dispute records are retained for the periods required by applicable law and payment-provider rules.

Your privacy rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection, and to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Send requests to support@raidbench.com. Identity verification may be required before account or transaction data is disclosed or changed.

Children

RaidBench is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. A parent or guardian can contact support to request review and deletion.

Policy changes

Material updates will be published on this page with a revised date. Changes apply prospectively unless applicable law requires otherwise.

RaidBench is an independent, unofficial player resource.
