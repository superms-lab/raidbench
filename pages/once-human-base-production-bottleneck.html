Why is my base producing less than the amount of activity suggests?

Direct answer

Measure one complete output from input gathering to finished storage. The constraint may be power, transfer, storage, processing time, placement, or player intervention. Improve the verified slowest stage before expanding the whole facility.

Decision signalWhat to verifyNext move
Inputs are abundantCheck where materials waitImprove transport or processing rather than gathering
Power is unstableMap consumers and peak loadProtect the production line that serves the next goal
Manual work dominatesCount repeated player interventionsSimplify the highest-attention step

Decision checklist

  • Choose one finished resource to measure
  • Map every stage from input to final storage
  • Record power, storage, processing, and manual delays
  • Change only the slowest verified stage
  • Compare total output over the same period

Stop and recheck when

  • Do not expand while the current bottleneck is unknown
  • Reverse changes that move congestion without increasing finished output

Common mistakes

  • Adding more inputs to a blocked processor
  • Expanding storage without improving transfer
  • Changing power and layout together before measuring
