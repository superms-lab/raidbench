Which parts of our Siege plan need changing after this patch?

Direct answer

Read the official notes, identify which operator or map interaction the plan depends on, and retest that exact decision. Keep unaffected setup and role assignments until evidence shows they no longer perform their job.

Decision signalWhat to verifyNext move
Operator ability changedTrace the affected team job and counterRetest the interaction before replacing the operator
Map or site changedReview rotations, sight lines, and setup timingUpdate only the broken geometry-dependent branch
Ranked rule changedMap it to lineup and round planningAdjust the decision threshold, not unrelated mechanics

Decision checklist

  • Read the current Ubisoft patch and designer notes
  • List changed operators, gadgets, maps, and rules used by the plan
  • Predict one measurable round impact
  • Repeat the affected setup or attack sequence
  • Update only the dependency whose outcome changed

Stop and recheck when

  • Stop when the claim is missing from current official notes
  • Do not change lineup, setup, and loadouts in one comparison

Common mistakes

  • Rebuilding from tier-list reactions
  • Treating every balance change as universal
  • Using community summaries as the factual source
