Which part of my Once Human build is actually holding it back?

Direct answer

Test the build in the activity it is meant to complete and record the first repeatable failure. Repair one layer at a time: damage delivery, survivability, resource uptime, encounter mechanics, or execution. Avoid changing every item after one bad result.

Decision signalWhat to verifyNext move
Damage is inconsistentCheck conditions, uptime, and target contextRepair the missing interaction before adding raw stats
Survival ends attemptsIdentify the attack or status causing failureAdd a targeted defensive response
Build is unaffordablePrice calibration, mods, and replacementsStage upgrades by measured impact

Decision checklist

  • Define the target activity and current version
  • Record the first repeatable failure
  • Classify the failure as damage, survival, resource, mechanic, or execution
  • Change one component and repeat the same test
  • Keep the previous build until the improvement repeats

Stop and recheck when

  • Stop when the test target or conditions change between runs
  • Reject upgrades whose resource cost exceeds their measured effect

Common mistakes

  • Replacing the complete build without a diagnosis
  • Testing only under perfect effect uptime
  • Spending scarce resources on several uncertain upgrades
