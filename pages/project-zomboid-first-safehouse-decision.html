What makes an early safehouse worth committing to?

Direct answer

Choose the location that supports several safe daily loops and at least two exits. A larger building is not automatically safer: travel exposure, poor sight lines, difficult clearing, and one-way access can make an impressive base expensive to maintain.

Decision signalWhat to verifyNext move
Short supply runsMap food, water, tools, and medical accessPrefer repeatable low-exposure routes
Surprise pressureIdentify two exits and fallback buildingsAvoid a base with one trapped approach
Long-term expansionCheck storage, vehicles, farming, and noiseCommit only after a short trial stay

Decision checklist

  • Clear and inspect the immediate perimeter
  • Walk two independent routes to essential supplies
  • Confirm more than one practical exit
  • Spend one night before moving irreplaceable equipment
  • Keep a small fallback cache away from the main building

Stop and recheck when

  • Leave if daily travel repeatedly forces high-risk crossings
  • Do not commit if clearing the perimeter consumes more resources than the location saves

Common mistakes

  • Choosing only for building size
  • Ignoring vehicle access and blocked exits
  • Moving every valuable item before testing the area
