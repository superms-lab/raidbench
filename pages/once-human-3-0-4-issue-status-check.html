Once Human 3.0.4 Issue Status Check: Fixed, Unresolved, or Intended?

Classify selected Once Human 3.0.4 symptoms as listed for correction, unresolved, console-specific, or intended before troubleshooting.

A symptom after an update does not automatically mean your installation or settings are at fault. Match it to the documented platform and status first.

Short answer

Use the August 31 version 3.0.4 notice as a dated classification record, not a complete statement of current status. It listed hairstyle dyeing after switching appearance presets or outfits, incorrect update-pending indicators on some appearance presets, Arachsiam combat-region teleporting, and continued console movement after stick input for correction. Classify the symptom before changing anything

Separate documented issue status from assumptions about the player's device, settings, or installation.

  • Record the platform, displayed game version, symptom, location, and the action that triggers it.
  • Match both the behavior and its qualifier; a console-only entry does not establish the same status on PC or mobile.
  • Treat the August 31 notice as a limited list rather than a complete catalog of version 3.0.4 issues.

Issues listed for correction in the August 31 notice

Summarize only the corrected categories contained in the supplied announcement while preserving their scope.

  • Hairstyles with exclusive color palettes could fail to accept dye after switching between appearance presets and outfits.
  • Some appearance presets could incorrectly display an update-pending status.
  • Arachsiam could fail to teleport players correctly into the combat region.
  • On console, a character could continue moving after stick input.
  • The announcement said completion timing could vary, so the listing alone does not prove that every player had received the change at a particular moment.

The console character-darkening issue remained unresolved

Prevent players from mistaking an attempted correction for a completed one.

  • The notice said the earlier attempted correction had not worked as intended and the abnormal darkening issue was not fully resolved.
  • Its scope in the supplied evidence is console only.
  • Recheck newer official notices before deciding whether the unresolved label still applies.
  • If it continues, capture the platform, version, appearance context, and reproduction sequence for an official report.

Frequently asked questions

Was the console character-darkening issue fixed by the August 31 notice?

No completed correction is established by that notice. It says an earlier attempt did not perform as expected and the issue was not fully resolved. Check newer official notices for its subsequent status.

Is the bicycle multiplayer-riding pose classified as a bug?

The August 31 notice describes it as intended multiplayer-riding behavior, while also recognizing that its presentation can be misunderstood. That classification does not cover other animation symptoms.

Can I assume every listed correction was active when the notice appeared?

No. The announcement says actual completion timing could vary. Confirm the installed version and retest the exact trigger before drawing a conclusion.

What if my symptom is absent from this guide?
