What should I calculate before attempting an important tame?

Direct answer

Confirm current server rates and creature behavior, then budget food, time, containment, protection, and the trip home. The attempt is ready only when a disruption does not force an improvised rescue with no supplies.

Decision checklist

  • Confirm the creature and current map availability
  • Record all server rates that affect the attempt
  • Bring a margin for food, tools, and interruptions
  • Prepare containment and a guarded staging area
  • Plan transport and space at the destination

Stop and recheck when

  • Abort when the creature behavior or server rate differs from the plan
  • Delay the attempt if there is no safe transport or destination

Common mistakes

  • Using official-rate estimates on a custom server
  • Starting before the perimeter is controlled
  • Planning the tame but not the trip home
