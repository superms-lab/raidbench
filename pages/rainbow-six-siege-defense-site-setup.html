What should a practical defense setup accomplish before the round starts?

Direct answer

The setup should create information and delay while preserving playable fallback positions. Assign every reinforcement, rotation, and gadget to a round purpose. A copied setup is weak when nobody knows which attack it is meant to resist.

Decision signalWhat to verifyNext move
Attack entry is predictableDefine information and first delay layerAssign utility and a safe fallback
Site needs movementMap rotations and protected retreat pathsAvoid openings that help attackers more
Utility is duplicatedAssign ownership and timingMove excess utility to an uncovered route

Decision checklist

  • Name the site's defensive win condition
  • Assign information and denial to likely entries
  • Create rotations with protected fallback positions
  • Confirm who owns each setup action
  • Review how the defense responds after the first layer fails

Stop and recheck when

  • Remove a setup element that improves attacker movement more than defense
  • Rebuild after map or operator updates change the interaction

Common mistakes

  • Copying a setup without its purpose
  • Opening rotations with no fallback plan
  • Stacking utility on one route while leaving another unobserved
