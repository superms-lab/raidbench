Where should the squad drop for a repeatable start?

Direct answer

Choose a location with a clear first objective and at least one workable rotation, not merely the highest loot reputation. Decide how many teams you are willing to contest and what the squad does when that limit is exceeded.

Decision signalWhat to verifyNext move
Squad wants early fightsSet the maximum contest and regroup pointLand together with a fast fallback
Squad wants placementCheck loot path, vehicle access, and first rotationPrefer repeatability over peak loot
Drop becomes overcrowdedIdentify a nearby secondary routeLeave before separate improvisation starts

Decision checklist

  • Define whether the session prioritizes fights, practice, or placement
  • Set an acceptable number of contesting teams
  • Mark a primary loot path and regroup point
  • Confirm vehicle and first-rotation options
  • Choose a secondary drop before the plane route appears

Stop and recheck when

  • Use the secondary plan when the contest exceeds the agreed limit
  • Abandon a drop that repeatedly offers no credible first rotation

Common mistakes

  • Choosing only by loot reputation
  • Landing across several buildings without a regroup plan
  • Waiting until the parachute phase to invent the fallback
