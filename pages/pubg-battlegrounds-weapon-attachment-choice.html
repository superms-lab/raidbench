Which weapon setup fits the fights I am actually taking?

Direct answer

Choose the pair that covers the team's expected ranges and that you can control with available ammunition and attachments. Compare complete roles rather than declaring one weapon universally best, and verify current patch behavior before using exact values.

Decision signalWhat to verifyNext move
Most fights are closeCheck handling, control, and ammunition burdenPair with a credible medium-range answer
Team lacks rangeDefine who handles distant pressureChoose a secondary that supports the squad
Attachment set is incompleteTest the available configurationAvoid judging the weapon only at ideal setup

Decision checklist

  • Define expected map, range, and squad role
  • Confirm current weapon and attachment behavior
  • Check ammunition and inventory burden
  • Test recoil with the attachments normally available
  • Keep one familiar fallback pair

Stop and recheck when

  • Reject exact comparisons that predate the current patch
  • Switch when the pair leaves a frequent engagement range uncovered

Common mistakes

  • Calling one weapon best across every range
  • Testing only with perfect attachments
  • Ignoring the squad's ammunition and role overlap
