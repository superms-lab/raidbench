What should I verify before committing to an extraction route?

Direct answer

An extraction is part of the plan only when its current availability and requirements are confirmed. Know the fallback, preserve enough time to reach it, and avoid routing every valuable raid through one conditional exit.

Decision signalWhat to verifyNext move
Exit may be conditionalConfirm its current requirement and statusCarry the requirement or choose another plan
Time margin is shrinkingEstimate travel plus one blocked approachLeave before the fallback disappears
Primary route is contestedIdentify a lower-value alternate exitProtect survival instead of defending the original plan

Decision checklist

  • Check the current map and mode-specific extraction list
  • Confirm every condition attached to the preferred exit
  • Choose a fallback on the opposite route branch
  • Reserve time for a blocked road or detour
  • Reassess immediately after completing the main objective

Stop and recheck when

  • Do not rely on an exit whose current conditions are uncertain
  • Switch routes when the time margin no longer protects the fallback

Common mistakes

  • Memorizing an old extraction without checking the patch
  • Arriving with no time for the extraction condition
  • Keeping the primary route after its assumptions fail
