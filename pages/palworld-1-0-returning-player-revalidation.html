Palworld 1.0 Returning Player Revalidation Checklist

Returning to Palworld after Early Access? Recheck mission progress, random-spawn data, Pal roles, progression goals, and resource plans for version 1.0.

Reviewed 2026-08-11Evidence-bounded Palworld guide; recheck after relevant patch changesEditorial standards

Palworld 1.0 changed enough systems that an old save may be familiar while your old plan is not. Revalidate the parts that affect your next decision before resuming the grind.

Short answer

Start by checking mission status rather than assuming every objective carried over: version 1.0 reset main- and sub-mission progress, while some tutorial and Tower Boss-related main missions may be marked complete based on prior Tower Boss defeats. Replace random-spawn placement data from v0.7.3 and earlier, then reassess Pal roles, routes, and progression priorities because habitats, Pal stats and abilities, Partner Skills, rewards, drops, and the player level cap changed.

What changed for returning players in Palworld 1.0

Establish the confirmed revalidation triggers without implying that every save element or previous strategy became invalid.

  • Main- and sub-mission progress was reset following reward adjustments.
  • Some tutorial and Tower Boss-related main missions may be marked complete according to prior Tower Boss defeat progress.
  • The player level cap increased from 65 to 80, and new technologies were added.
  • Pal habitats, stats, abilities, and the Partner Skills of over 200 Pals were reworked or rebalanced.
  • Treasure chests, dungeon rewards, item drops, dungeon layouts, and several exploration systems changed.

Audit your mission state before choosing a destination

Help returning players identify their actual story position from the current interface.

  • Open the mission screens and record which main missions, sub-missions, and tutorial objectives are currently active or complete.
  • Treat the displayed state as the starting point for planning instead of reconstructing it from Early Access memory.
  • If a Tower Boss-related mission appears complete, connect that status to recorded defeat progress without assuming the same exception applies elsewhere.
  • Choose the next displayed objective only after checking its destination markers and stated rewards.

Replace invalid random-spawn information

Prevent players using random Pal spawn mode from planning around placement data that version 1.0 no longer carries forward.

  • If random Pal spawn mode is enabled, retire placement notes or route maps based on v0.7.3 and earlier.
  • Scout the regions relevant to the current goal and create fresh observations before committing to a capture route.
  • Do not generalize this placement-data warning to every mode; the changelog states it specifically for random Pal spawn mode.

Revalidate Pal roles instead of copying an old team

Turn broad balance changes into a practical team review without unsupported rankings.

  • Assign each current Pal a job such as combat, support, travel, capture assistance, or base work.
  • Read current stats, abilities, and Partner Skill descriptions before retaining an old role assignment.
  • Test whether each Pal still performs the intended job under the current version.
  • Change one role at a time so the effect is observable.
  • Use current objectives and available resources as the decision criteria; there is no universally suitable choice.

Rebuild progression priorities around one next goal

Keep the returning-player review focused despite the expanded level cap, new areas, technologies, bosses, and exploration systems.

  • Select one near-term goal: mission progress, exploration, a boss attempt, base development, or team rebuilding.
  • List only the technologies, equipment, Pals, travel points, and materials needed for that goal.
  • Check whether new watchtowers, fast-travel points, settlements, islands, or dungeon entrances change the route.
  • Reassess the plan after completing the goal instead of redesigning the entire save at once.

Review rewards and resource routes cautiously

Address overlap with older resource and boss-preparation guidance while staying within the changelog evidence.

  • Do not assume an Early Access chest, dungeon, or item-drop route produces the same result in version 1.0.
  • Run a short observation pass before investing heavily in a remembered route.
  • For boss preparation, verify the current team roles and required supplies rather than relying on previous balance assumptions.
  • Keep useful general planning habits, but retest any recommendation that depends on habitats, rewards, drops, stats, abilities, or Partner Skills.

Decision checklist

  • Open the current mission screens and record active and completed objectives.
  • Check whether prior Tower Boss defeats affected any displayed mission completion.
  • Replace random-spawn placement data from v0.7.3 and earlier if that mode is enabled.
  • Choose one immediate progression goal.
  • Review the current role of every Pal needed for that goal.
  • Verify current stats, abilities, and Partner Skill descriptions for those Pals.
  • Test remembered reward and resource routes before committing substantial time.
  • Record the revised team, route, and next checkpoint.

Worked example

A returning player remembers preparing a team for a Tower Boss and using an old random-spawn capture route. They first check the mission screen and find that the relevant mission state does not match their old notes. Because random Pal spawn mode is enabled, they retire placement data from v0.7.3 and earlier. They then review the current roles and descriptions of the Pals intended for the fight, run a short route test for the required supplies, and change only the parts of the plan that current observations no longer support.

Common mistakes

  • Assuming every mission retained its Early Access status.
  • Assuming the limited conditional mission completion applies to all reset progress.
  • Using v0.7.3-or-earlier placement data in random Pal spawn mode.
  • Keeping old Pal role assignments without reading current abilities and Partner Skills.
  • Treating every previous route or preparation habit as invalid instead of testing patch-sensitive parts.
  • Trying to replan the entire save before selecting one immediate goal.

Frequently asked questions

Did Palworld 1.0 reset all progress?

The supplied changelog confirms resets to main- and sub-mission progress, with conditional completion for some tutorial and Tower Boss-related main missions based on Tower Boss defeat progress. It does not support a claim that all save progress was reset.

Can I keep using an old Pal location map?

If you use random Pal spawn mode, placement data from v0.7.3 and earlier is no longer carried over correctly after the habitat rebalance. The supplied evidence does not establish that the same warning applies to every game mode.

Do I need to replace my entire Pal team?

Not necessarily. Review each Pal against its current role because version 1.0 rebalanced Pal stats and abilities and reworked the Partner Skills of over 200 Pals. Keep choices that still meet the role after inspection and testing.

What should I do first after loading an old save?

Check the current mission state, choose one immediate goal, and then revalidate only the Pals, routes, rewards, and resources that affect that goal.

Are old farming and dungeon routes still reliable?

Treat them as unverified until tested. Version 1.0 rebalanced treasure chests, dungeon rewards, and item drops, while also changing dungeon and exploration features. The changelog does not specify the outcome for every individual route.
