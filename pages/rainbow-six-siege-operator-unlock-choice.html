Which operator should I unlock next for the way I play?

Direct answer

Choose the operator who solves a recurring team or site problem and whose job you can execute consistently. Compare role coverage, map frequency, teammate overlap, and current ability behavior instead of buying from a universal tier list.

Decision signalWhat to verifyNext move
Team repeatedly lacks a jobName the information, denial, breach, support, or control needShortlist operators that fill it
Several operators fitCompare map frequency and execution difficultyChoose the one likely to be played often
Tier list is persuasiveCheck its season and assumed squadReject rankings without the same context

Decision checklist

  • List the attack or defense jobs already covered
  • Identify the recurring missing job
  • Check current official operator behavior
  • Compare map and site frequency
  • Choose one operator and define a practice objective

Stop and recheck when

  • Delay the unlock when the reason is only a stale tier list
  • Reject an operator whose role already has reliable team coverage

Common mistakes

  • Buying only for weapon preference
  • Ignoring the maps and sites actually played
  • Treating a solo ranking as a coordinated-team answer
