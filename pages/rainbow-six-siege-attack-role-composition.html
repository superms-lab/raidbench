Does this attacking lineup cover the jobs the site requires?

Direct answer

Start from the site plan and list the jobs required to reach and hold the objective. Choose operators after information, breach, denial removal, entry, flank control, and plant responsibilities are assigned. Avoid five useful operators with no shared sequence.

Decision signalWhat to verifyNext move
Site route is selectedList walls, denial, information, and flank risksAssign one operator or tool to each essential job
Lineup has strong fraggingCheck breach and objective executionReplace overlap with the missing route function
First plan is deniedDefine a time-based fallback routeSwitch before the remaining clock removes execution

Decision checklist

  • Choose the intended site and entry route
  • Assign information, entry, breach, and denial-removal jobs
  • Define flank control and plant ownership
  • Check current operator and gadget interactions
  • Set a fallback route and switch time

Stop and recheck when

  • Change the lineup when an essential breach or denial job is uncovered
  • Abandon the first route when the fallback time is reached

Common mistakes

  • Choosing operators before choosing a site plan
  • Overlapping frag roles while missing breach
  • Waiting until the final seconds to change routes
