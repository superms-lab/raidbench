PUBG position review before the final gunfight

Which earlier decision made this position difficult to survive?

Direct answer

Work backward from the final position to the first moment the squad lost a safer option. Review timing, information, cover, spacing, and vehicle decisions before the final shot. Change one repeated route or positioning habit.

Decision checklist

  • Record the zone and squad objective
  • Find the first lost route or cover option
  • Check information available at that moment
  • Review spacing, vehicles, and terrain
  • Choose one earlier decision to test next match

Stop and recheck when

  • Stop when no replay or reliable sequence is available
  • Do not change the whole drop plan from one unusual fight

Common mistakes

  • Reviewing only the final spray
  • Ignoring the rotation that created the position
  • Assigning blame without checking squad spacing
