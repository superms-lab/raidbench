Tarkov stash keep-or-sell triage

How do I decide what to keep instead of filling the stash?

Direct answer

Keep items tied to a near-term verified use, expensive replacement, or constrained access. Sell duplicates and speculative stock that blocks functional kits. The goal is not maximum theoretical value; it is a stash that supports the next several decisions.

Decision signalWhat to verifyNext move
Needed soonMatch the item to a current task or upgradeKeep only the required quantity
Easy to replaceConfirm repeatable trader or raid accessConvert excess storage into usable funds
Value is speculativeName the exact future use and timingSell when the plan is only a possibility

Decision checklist

  • List the next three tasks or upgrades
  • Tag items required for those confirmed goals
  • Check current trader and crafting access before hoarding
  • Set quantity limits for common duplicates
  • Preserve enough space to assemble complete raid kits

Stop and recheck when

  • Stop hoarding when storage pressure prevents normal preparation
  • Recheck items whose value depends on an outdated task or recipe

Common mistakes

  • Keeping every item mentioned by an old checklist
  • Valuing items without considering storage cost
  • Selling constrained task items before checking near-term requirements
