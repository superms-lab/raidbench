What should I check before taking a vehicle on a long supply run?

Direct answer

Prepare the return before planning the loot. Check the vehicle, fuel margin, route alternatives, repair needs, and what can be abandoned. The trip is ready when one blocked road or damaged part does not erase the entire run.

Decision signalWhat to verifyNext move
Route is unfamiliarMark a primary and fallback roadTurn back before fuel removes the choice
Vehicle is essentialInspect condition and carry relevant toolsAvoid using the only working vehicle as a gamble
Cargo may be heavyReserve space for recovery suppliesDefine what gets left behind first

Decision checklist

  • Inspect critical vehicle condition before loading
  • Carry fuel and recovery tools appropriate to the plan
  • Mark at least one alternate road and safe stopping point
  • Reserve inventory space for emergency movement
  • Set a fuel or damage threshold that triggers the return

Stop and recheck when

  • Return when the remaining fuel no longer protects a route change
  • Abandon optional cargo before risking the only transport

Common mistakes

  • Planning cargo capacity but not the return journey
  • Taking the only functioning vehicle without a fallback
  • Continuing after the route and vehicle assumptions both fail
