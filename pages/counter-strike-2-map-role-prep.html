What should I prepare for a consistent role on one map?

Direct answer

Prepare the first thirty seconds and the first recovery decision. Know the opening information goal, required utility, teammate spacing, fallback, and what new information changes the plan. A role is a decision package, not a fixed hiding place.

Decision signalWhat to verifyNext move
Opening contact occursDefine information and trade responsibilityTake the duel only with the planned support
No contact occursSet the timing for rotation or informationAvoid remaining irrelevant to the round
Plan is counteredName the fallback position and utilityPreserve value for the mid-round

Decision checklist

  • Name the map side and normal role
  • Define the opening information goal
  • Assign utility and trade spacing
  • Choose a fallback position and rotation trigger
  • Review one round where the role became irrelevant

Stop and recheck when

  • Change the role when a map update removes the position or timing
  • Stop repeating an opening the opponent consistently counters

Common mistakes

  • Treating the role as one location
  • Taking opening contact without trade spacing
  • Having no action when the expected contact never arrives
