Should the team contest this objective right now?

Direct answer

Contest when the team has a clear win condition, enough information, relevant resources, and a useful result after the fight. If those conditions are missing, trade map value, delay, or preserve the next timing instead of arriving late and divided.

Decision signalWhat to verifyNext move
Team timing is activeCheck items, ultimates, health, and map positionGroup early with a defined fight plan
Information is poorIdentify enemy locations and approach routesDelay or trade until the risk is bounded
Objective cannot be contestedName the opposite-side valueTrade immediately rather than watching the loss

Decision checklist

  • Confirm the objective timing and current map state
  • Check key items, cooldowns, resources, and buybacks
  • Identify enemy information and approach routes
  • Define the fight win condition
  • Choose the trade before the contest becomes impossible

Stop and recheck when

  • Do not contest when the team arrives in separate waves
  • Leave when the original fight condition has already expired

Common mistakes

  • Walking toward every objective by default
  • Checking items but not map position
  • Failing to take a trade when the contest is impossible
