What did I do wrong in this match, beyond the final team fight?

Direct answer

Start with the role the hero needed to perform, then find the earliest decision that made that job harder. Review lane resources, information, item timing, and objective options before judging the final fight or copying a different build.

Decision signalWhat to verifyNext move
Hero job was unclearDefine the draft's damage, control, or objective needJudge choices against that job
Lane became unstableFind the first lost resource or unsafe waveRepair the earliest repeatable decision
Final fight looked decisiveReview vision, items, and objective state beforehandChange the setup before the execution

Decision checklist

  • Write the intended role in one sentence
  • Review the first ten minutes for resources and avoidable risks
  • Mark major item timings for both teams
  • Identify the first lost objective option
  • Choose one earlier decision to test next match

Stop and recheck when

  • Stop making claims when the replay or match context is unavailable
  • Do not rebuild the hero from one unusual draft

Common mistakes

  • Reviewing only deaths and kills
  • Treating the recommended item build as context-free
  • Changing several habits after one match
