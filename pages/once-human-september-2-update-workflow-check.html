Once Human September 2 Update: Download, Storage Search, and Portable Crafting Check

Prepare for the Once Human September 2 update, verify storage search, and test RaidZone portable crafting without prematurely changing established routines.

Reviewed 2026-09-04Evidence-bounded Once Human guide; recheck after relevant patch changesEditorial standards

Treat the September 2 update as three separate jobs: prepare the download, verify the new interfaces, and change established routines only after a controlled test.

Short answer

Before updating, use the platform-specific estimates in the official announcement to reserve download time and storage headroom. Steam users should also allow for the stated possibility of duplicate downloads if the platform push is delayed. After entering the game, verify storage search in a storage crate or Storage Terminal. In RaidZone, test backpack-based portable crafting with one item that previously required a campsite. Keep the campsite in your routine because the announcement says it remains a temporary respawn point and cooking area. Expand any workflow change only after the feature works in the mode and context you intend to play.

Prepare the update before changing anything in game

Separate download logistics from gameplay changes so an installation issue does not get mistaken for a changed mechanic.

  • Identify the platform and whether its official estimate distinguishes launcher and in-game downloads.
  • Reserve sufficient device space, connection time, and session margin; the announcement notes that actual size may vary.
  • On Steam, account for the stated possibility of duplicate downloads when the platform push is delayed.
  • Record the scenario or mode whose workflow you plan to test after installation.

Verify storage search with a known-item test

Confirm the announced search interface without assuming that it changes storage capacity, transfer behavior, or production output.

  • Open a storage crate or Storage Terminal after the update.
  • Search for an item whose storage location you already know.
  • Confirm that the result identifies the expected stored item before relying on search during reorganizing.
  • Treat search as a locating aid; the supplied announcement does not establish changes to throughput or storage capacity.

Test portable crafting inside RaidZone

Validate the documented scope before removing established campsite steps from a routine.

  • Enter RaidZone and open the portable crafting entry from the backpack.
  • Choose one item that previously required crafting at a campsite.
  • Complete a low-cost test and record whether the expected item and operation are available.
  • Do not generalize the result to other modes because the announcement says broader availability is planned for the future.

Keep the remaining campsite functions visible

Prevent a new crafting option from being interpreted as a complete campsite replacement.

  • Retain the campsite where its temporary respawn function matters.
  • Retain access to its cooking function when the route depends on it.
  • Remove only the crafting trip that the RaidZone test actually replaces.
  • Restore the prior route if the tested item, mode, or interface does not match the intended use.

Decide whether to revise the routine

Turn the feature checks into a bounded workflow decision.

  • Adopt storage search when it reduces time spent locating known materials.
  • Adopt portable crafting only for the tested RaidZone item and context.
  • Keep unaffected production, storage, and campsite steps unchanged.
  • Recheck the official scope after a later update before extending the routine to another mode.

Decision checklist

  • Confirm the device platform and update method.
  • Review the matching launcher and in-game download estimates in the official announcement.
  • Reserve extra time and storage headroom for size variation.
  • If using Steam, allow for the stated duplicate-download possibility.
  • Record the scenario or mode that will be tested.
  • Search for one known item in a storage crate or Storage Terminal.
  • In RaidZone, craft one eligible item from the backpack.
  • Keep the campsite available for temporary respawn and cooking functions until the revised route is proven.

Worked example

A Steam player plans a RaidZone session and normally returns to a campsite to craft a particular eligible item. Before the update, the player reviews the official PC estimates and leaves additional time and device space because the notice says actual size may vary and duplicate Steam downloads may occur if the platform push is delayed. After installation, the player searches for a known material in a storage crate, then enters RaidZone and crafts one previously campsite-required item through the backpack. The player removes that single crafting detour from the route but keeps the campsite for temporary respawn and cooking. No other base or inventory step changes until it receives its own test.

Common mistakes

  • Using one version number in the page title while the supplied official sources disagree on the label.
  • Assuming storage search changes capacity, transfer speed, or production output.
  • Removing a campsite before accounting for its remaining temporary respawn and cooking functions.
  • Extending RaidZone portable-crafting behavior to other modes before official availability is confirmed.
  • Changing storage organization and crafting routes simultaneously, making the source of any improvement unclear.

Frequently asked questions

Is the September 2 update Version 3.0.4 or Version 3.0.5?

The closed official evidence is inconsistent: the Steam announcement is titled Version 3.0.5, while the official update listing identifies the September 2 announcement as Version 3.0.4. This guide therefore identifies it by date and leaves the version label under review.

Does storage search work in both crates and Storage Terminals?

The announcement states that search was added to storage crates and Storage Terminals. Test with a known stored item before reorganizing around the feature.

Does storage search improve production output?

The supplied evidence supports faster item location, but it does not establish a change to storage capacity, transfer behavior, or production output. Use it as a diagnostic and navigation aid.

Can portable crafting replace my campsite in RaidZone?

It can replace the campsite crafting trip for supported items that previously required campsite crafting, but the campsite remains a temporary respawn point and cooking area.

Is portable crafting available outside RaidZone?

The announcement documents current availability in RaidZone and says it will reach other modes in the future. Do not assume availability in another mode until a current official notice or an in-game check confirms it.

Why should Steam players leave extra update time?

The announcement says duplicate downloads may occur if the Steam platform push is delayed. This is a possibility rather than a certainty, so leave a reasonable margin without treating it as an installation failure.
