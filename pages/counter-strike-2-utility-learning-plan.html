CS2 utility learning plan for one map

Which grenades should I learn first on a CS2 map?

Direct answer

Choose utility that solves the rounds your role repeatedly encounters: one entry route, one defensive delay, one retake, and one recovery option. Practice the throw and the decision that triggers it, then add more only after it appears in matches.

Decision signalWhat to verifyNext move
Entry repeatedly stallsIdentify the sight line or position causing itLearn one reliable piece that changes the duel
Defense lacks timeChoose a delay or escape usePractice the timing with the fallback path
Lineup is rarely usedTrack whether the role reaches that situationReplace it with a higher-frequency problem

Decision checklist

  • Choose one map and normal team role
  • List the three round problems seen most often
  • Learn one simple utility answer for each
  • Practice position, aim point, timing, and fallback
  • Track actual match use before adding another lineup

Stop and recheck when

  • Drop a lineup that depends on an outdated map position
  • Do not add more utility while the current set cannot be recalled under pressure

Common mistakes

  • Memorizing lineups without trigger conditions
  • Practicing only perfect stationary throws
  • Learning utility unrelated to the player's role
