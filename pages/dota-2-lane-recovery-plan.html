What should I do when the lane is already going wrong?

Direct answer

Stop measuring recovery only by winning the lane back. Protect the hero's next useful timing, secure the safest available resources, and coordinate whether the team contests, trades, or abandons the next objective. Avoid feeding the same failed pattern.

Decision signalWhat to verifyNext move
Lane remains playableCheck wave position, support resources, and kill threatStabilize without forcing a comeback fight
Lane denies all progressIdentify alternate safe resources and rotationsProtect the next item or level timing
Enemy converts the advantageMap the next tower or objectiveContest only with a credible team condition

Decision checklist

  • Describe why the lane is losing
  • Check wave, levels, resources, and enemy kill threat
  • Identify the next useful hero timing
  • Choose safe resources or a purposeful rotation
  • Tell the team which objective can be defended or traded

Stop and recheck when

  • Stop returning to a lane that cannot produce safe progress
  • Do not rotate without a resource or objective purpose

Common mistakes

  • Trying to erase the deficit with one forced fight
  • Leaving the lane without a next resource plan
  • Ignoring the enemy's first conversion objective
