Why does a build that looks strong still fail in the target mission?

Direct answer

Name the exact failure and the mission context before replacing mods. A build may have enough theoretical damage but fail to apply it, maintain resources, survive the objective, or recover after disruption. Change one diagnosed layer at a time.

Decision signalWhat to verifyNext move
Enemies take too longSeparate raw damage from application and uptimeTest one damage interaction at a time
Deaths interrupt the loopIdentify the attack, status, or resource failureAdd the smallest reliable defensive repair
Mission objective failsCheck crowd control, mobility, and coverageOptimize for the objective rather than a dummy target

Decision checklist

  • Record mission type, enemy context, and current patch
  • Describe the first repeatable failure in one sentence
  • Classify it as damage, survival, resource, objective, or execution
  • Change one mod or interaction and repeat the same test
  • Keep the previous configuration until the improvement repeats

Stop and recheck when

  • Stop when the test environment changes between comparisons
  • Reject a repair that improves one number while worsening the mission result

Common mistakes

  • Replacing the build before diagnosing the failure
  • Testing only in a favorable simulation
  • Stacking damage while resource uptime collapses
