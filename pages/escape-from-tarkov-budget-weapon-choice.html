What should be my go-to gun at my current level and budget?

Direct answer

The best default is the platform you can replace with ammunition that suits the intended target and a configuration you can control. Compare complete recurring cost and access, not an isolated weapon statistic or a streamer build.

Decision signalWhat to verifyNext move

Decision checklist

  • Define the map, task, and expected engagement distance
  • Confirm current ammunition and magazine access
  • Build the complete kit and record its replacement cost
  • Test recoil and handling before risking several copies
  • Keep a cheaper fallback using supplies already available

Stop and recheck when

  • Reject the build if ammunition cannot be replenished consistently
  • Downgrade when one loss would prevent the next required task attempt

Common mistakes

  • Choosing a gun without checking ammunition access
  • Comparing base weapon prices instead of complete kits
  • Copying a build that assumes higher trader access
