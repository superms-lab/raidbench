Which decision actually caused this Siege round to collapse?

Direct answer

Find the first point where the team lost information, utility, spacing, time, or a fallback route. Review the round in sequence and change one repeatable decision. The final duel often exposes an earlier planning failure rather than creating it.

Decision checklist

  • Record site, lineup, and intended plan
  • Find the first meaningful information event
  • Review utility and spacing at that moment
  • Identify the first lost fallback or timing
  • Choose one decision trigger to change next round

Stop and recheck when

  • Stop when no replay or reliable sequence is available
  • Do not replace the full setup after one unusual attacker plan

Common mistakes

  • Reviewing only the final kill
  • Counting unused utility without asking when it was needed
  • Changing several roles from one round
