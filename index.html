Upkeep basics

Use the daily values shown in your Tool Cupboard to establish a weekly material target. Add a practical reserve for expansion, missed farming sessions, and emergency repairs.

  • Keep one day of emergency upkeep before roaming or raiding.
  • Track stone and metal separately for honeycomb and doors.
  • Use weekly totals before committing to a larger footprint.

Early-game raid paths

Early raids reward disciplined target selection. Compare door costs, wall routes, available explosives, exposure time, and the resources required to seal the breach.
