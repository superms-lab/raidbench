Quick answers, current assumptions

Rust raid-cost questions worth checking before you craft

How much sulfur does one rocket cost?

The current vanilla Rust PC recipe roll-up is 1,400 sulfur per rocket. RaidBench checks the monitored recipe and target counts before releasing paid answers.

How many rockets does a sheet metal door take?

The current RaidBench vanilla reference uses two rockets for one sheet metal door. A mixed method can be cheaper, so compare the complete route instead of one layer.

Should I use C4 or rockets?

C4 concentrates damage on an isolated target. Rockets become more valuable when their splash reaches another useful building piece. The correct comparison is the full route, not one item.

Can I share a RaidBench route with my team?

Yes. Build the route in the calculator and use Copy share link. The link restores targets, quantities, methods, and totals without exposing account information.
